ProfessionalBench website assets

Manuscript and figures
- Supplied by the project owner: humanlaya_professionalbench (35).pdf.
- Figures 1–14 are displayed on the site; the complete manuscript includes Figures 1–17.
- All 14 displayed figures use SVG vector paths extracted from the supplied manuscript with Poppler. No displayed SVG contains an embedded raster image.
- Figure 12 replaces only raster decorative card backgrounds with SVG rectangles. Original labels, data, and chart geometry are retained.
- Small raster decorations in Figure 1 (cycle arrows), Figure 2 (shadows and hierarchical task-space decoration), and Figure 5 (color bar) are replaced with native SVG geometry. Figure 2 decorative shadows are simplified; the source data and substantive figure content are retained.
- Older WebP crops remain in the asset archive but are not displayed on the page.
- The page uses a text wordmark for Humanlaya Data; the previously supplied bitmap identity remains in the asset archive.

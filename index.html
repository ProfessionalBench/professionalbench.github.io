03 / The idea

Preserve every responsibility.

Workflow Compression occurs when task construction omits or pre-resolves responsibilities the designated professional role should fulfill independently.

K01 / JUDGMENT

Know

Assess evidence. Form judgments.

Independently discover and assess evidence, determine its applicability, and form grounded professional judgments.

A02 / EXECUTION

Act

Plan, execute, and adapt.

Apply judgments through professional execution and respond to intermediate outcomes, constraints, and exceptions.

C03 / CLOSURE

Close

Verify and substantiate completion.

Check the actual outcomes, address in-scope discrepancies, and substantiate completion with traceable evidence.

Coupled responsibilities—not three independent subtasks or a prescribed one-pass sequence. §3.2 ↗

06 / Evaluation

Check the evidence.
Separate completion from acceptance.

Expert-reviewed Dense Rubrics link professional responsibilities to observable evidence in deliverables, required work records, and business states.

EVIDENCE

Hybrid verification

Programmatic verifiers check deterministic conditions. An Agent Verifier checks open-ended professional requirements against frozen evidence.

REQUIREMENT COMPLETION

Outcome Score

Signed, weighted binary criteria measure fulfillment. Penalties reduce the score; a triggered hurdle sets the task score to zero.

PROFESSIONAL ACCEPTANCE

Professional Pass

Acceptance requires a task score of at least 85 and satisfaction of every critical requirement. It is stricter than partial completion.

Evaluation is grounded in submitted artifacts and observable evidence, not self-reported success. §3.5 · evaluation protocol ↗
